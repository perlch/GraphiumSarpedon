Why Graphium

Notes that stay sharp, forever

Most note-taking apps render ink as a flat raster image — it loses sharpness when you zoom, and is hard to edit cleanly afterward. Graphium keeps every stroke, shape and equation as real, resolution-independent SVG geometry, stored as plain files on your own disk.
